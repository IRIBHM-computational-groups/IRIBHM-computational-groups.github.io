Internal resources

Computing infrastructure & HPC guidelines

Our groups run their analyses on shared high-performance computing infrastructure hosted at the institute. To help everyone use it well, we maintain a living set of internal guidelines, written and kept up to date by lab members themselves.

These guidelines are internal to the lab and are not published here, but here is what they cover.

🚀

Getting started

How to obtain an account, connect to our servers, and find your way around the storage and software environment during your first days.

⚙️

Running analyses

How to use the available compute resources, work with containers for reproducible environments, and run long jobs without losing your work.

🗄️

Data & reference resources

Where shared datasets and reference databases live, and how to organise and store your own project data.

📏

Rules of the shared environment

The etiquette that keeps a shared system usable for everyone, from resource use to data handling and security.

✅

Good computational practices

Our conventions for reproducible analyses, version control, and preparing scientific code for publication, along with day-to-day tips.

🛠️

Maintenance & tooling

How our internal tools and websites are maintained, so that anyone in the lab can keep them running and improve them.

💬 A dedicated Teams channel

A Teams channel is in place for everything computing-related. New members are added when they arrive and find the link to the guidelines there. It is also the place to ask questions, report problems with the servers, and share solutions: no question is too basic, and answers benefit everybody.

🤝 A lab member at your side

Every newcomer is paired with an experienced member of the lab who helps them get set up during their first weeks: accounts and access, first connection to the servers, running a first analysis, and all the practical details that are quicker to learn with someone next to you.

Joining the lab, or already a member and need access? Get in touch with your group leader or ask on the Teams channel. Group leaders are listed on our people page.
